Israel Radio - Station Scanner (utility)

Standalone maintainer tool, not part of the app itself and not linked from it - safe to ignore or delete. Fetches Israel-tagged stations from the community-run Radio-Browser database, tests each one live in your browser with your real connection, and lets you export just the working ones as JSON to hand back for the app's built-in candidate list.
